The data product offers the contract as its output port, in the Open Data Product Standard. Linted and published with the Data Product CLI.

        

        


slow-selling-brands/
├── dataproduct.odps.yaml
├── datacontracts/
│   └── slow-selling-brands.odcs.yaml
├── models/
│   ├── input_ports/      # carry their contracts and source descriptions
│   │   ├── articles_latest.yml
│   │   └── shelf_warmers.yml
│   ├── intermediate/
│   └── output_port/      # the new contract and the SQL
│       ├── slow_selling_brands.sql
│       └── slow_selling_brands.yml
└── dbt_project.yml

          
Laid out by the dataproduct-bootstrap skill, implemented by dataproduct-implement. Simplified.

        

        


with shelf_warmers as (select * from {{ ref('shelf_warmers') }}),
     articles      as (select * from {{ ref('articles_latest') }})

select
  a.brand_name::varchar         as brand,
  count(*)::integer            as slow_selling_products,
  max(sw.last_sold_date)::date as latest_sold_date
from shelf_warmers sw
join articles a on sw.sku = a.sku   -- same concept in the semantics
group by a.brand_name

          
Joined on SKU, because both columns link to the same concept. Simplified.
